SERVICE AGREEMENT
This Service Agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between Veda Technologies Pvt. Ltd., Hyderabad (the "Service Provider") and Nila Retail LLP, Bengaluru (the "Client").
1. Services. The Service Provider shall deliver the software development services described in Schedule A by 31 December 2026.
2. Payment. The Client shall pay a fee of ₹12,50,000 in three instalments. Invoices must be paid within 15 days of receipt. Late payments will attract a penalty of 2% per month.
3. Confidentiality. Each party shall keep all Confidential Information secret and must not disclose it to any third party for 5 years after termination.
4. Intellectual Property. All work product shall be owned by the Client upon full payment, and the Service Provider irrevocably waives all moral rights.
5. Liability. The Service Provider's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited, and the Service Provider shall indemnify the Client against all losses, claims and expenses.
6. Term and Renewal. This Agreement runs for 12 months and shall automatically renew for successive 12-month terms unless cancelled in writing 60 days before expiry.
7. Termination. The Client may terminate this Agreement at its sole discretion without notice. The Service Provider may terminate only for material breach with 30 days' written notice.
8. Non-Compete. The Service Provider shall not provide similar services to any competitor of the Client for 24 months.
9. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Hyderabad, Telangana.
